Privacy Policy

AI Artist Publisher · last updated 7 October 2026

AI Artist Publisher is a private tool used by the owner of the "AI Artist" Facebook Page and the matching Instagram account to publish the owner's own videos to those two accounts. It is not offered to anyone else, and nobody else can sign in to it.

What data the app uses

The app only uses the access the owner granted for his own Facebook Page and Instagram account, to upload his own videos and their captions. It does not read, collect, store or share any information about other people, including people who view, like or comment on the posts.

What is stored

The only things kept are the owner's own access token, on the owner's own computer, and the links to the posts the app published. Nothing is sold, shared with third parties, or used for advertising.

Data deletion

The app holds no data about any user other than the owner. If you believe it holds data about you, or want anything removed, send a message to the AI Artist Facebook Page and it will be deleted. The owner can remove the app's access at any time in Facebook Settings → Business Integrations.

Contact

Send a message to the AI Artist Page on Facebook.
